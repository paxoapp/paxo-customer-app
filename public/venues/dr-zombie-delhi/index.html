Dr. Zombie

Restaurant · Connaught Place, Delhi

35, C Block, First Floor, Inner Circle, Connaught Place, New Delhi

View on Google Maps

3 unlimited party packages from ₹1,200 per person, for groups of up to 220 guests.

Dr. Zombie in Connaught Place's Inner Circle is a quirky food, coffee and drink lab with a wild zombie theme, exposed brick interiors, a lively bar and a private dining room. Bring your group for global food, tiki cocktails and a full bar, with a DJ keeping the energy up.
